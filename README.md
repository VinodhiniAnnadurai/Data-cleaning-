Product Data Management Dataset
Overview
This project contains a product dataset with information about product IDs,
manufacturing dates, 
country codes,
product names,
brands, prices,
quantities, 
and categories.

Dataset Details
The dataset includes the following fields:

Product ID – Unique product identifier.

Manufacturing Date – Product manufacturing date.
Country Code – Country identifier (US, UK, IN).
Product Name – Original product name.
Clean Product Name – Standardized product name.
Brand Name – Product manufacturer or brand.
Product Brand – Combined brand and product name.
Price – Product price.
Missing Price – Field for identifying missing price values.
Quantity – Available product quantity.
Category – Product classification.
Missing Category – Field for identifying missing categories.
Objectives
Organize product information.
Standardize product names.
Identify missing prices and categories.
Maintain consistent date formats.
Support product data cleaning and analysis.
